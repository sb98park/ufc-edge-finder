Short version: the trial exists so you can decide before paying anything. If you are charged and did not mean to be, email us within 14 days and we will refund it. Cancel any time and you keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

The trial comes first

Every new member gets a free trial, timed to cover at least one full fight card. Cancel before it ends and you are never charged. This is deliberate: we would rather you left during the trial than paid for something that turned out not to be for you.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from your account, in one step, with no email required and nobody trying to talk you out of it. Cancellation stops future billing. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for — we do not cut you off the moment you cancel.

Refunds

  • Within 14 days of a charge: email us and we will refund it in full. You do not need to explain why.
  • After 14 days: we do not refund periods already elapsed, but you can cancel so you are not billed again.
  • Annual plans: the same 14-day window applies from the date of each renewal charge.
  • Extended outage: if the service is substantially unavailable for a stretch of your billing period, tell us and we will refund or credit that time, whether or not 14 days have passed.

Before an annual renewal

We email you before an annual subscription renews, with the amount and the date, so a yearly charge is never a surprise. If that email does not reach you and you did not intend to renew, tell us — we will treat it as within the refund window.

What we will not refund

Losing a bet is not grounds for a refund. We sell analysis, not outcomes, and the model is wrong a meaningful share of the time — the published track record shows exactly how often. If that is not a product you want, the trial is the right moment to decide.

How to ask

Email support@octanealpha.com from the address on the account. We reply within two business days, and refunds reach your bank within 5–10 days once issued.

Please ask us before disputing a charge

If something looks wrong, email us first. A refund we issue directly reaches you faster than a chargeback, and disputes are costly for a small operation to contest. We would rather simply give you your money back.
