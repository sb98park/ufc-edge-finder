This describes exactly what Octane Alpha collects and what happens to it. It is short because the answer is genuinely small.

There are no third-party trackers on this site. No Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording, no data brokers. Every script the site runs is served from our own domain. We have never sold or shared personal data and have no plans to.

What we collect

  • Your email address, because it is how you sign in.
  • An account identifier issued by our authentication provider.
  • Subscription status — whether you are on a trial, active, or cancelled, and when the current period ends.
  • A Stripe customer identifier, so we can match a payment to an account.
  • Ordinary server logs at our edge provider: IP address, user agent, and which pages were requested. These are used to keep the service working and are not linked to your account.

What we do not collect

  • Card numbers. Payment details go directly to Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Any record of what you bet, whether you bet, or with whom.
  • Behavioural profiles, cross-site activity, or anything for advertising.
  • Your name, address or phone number, unless you volunteer it by emailing us.

Cookies

Three, all first-party, all necessary for the service to function. None are used for advertising or analytics.

  • octane_session — proves you are signed in and whether you have an active subscription. Expires after 30 minutes.
  • octane_auth and octane_role — used by the temporary access gate during our private launch period. These disappear once the site is public.

The site also stores one item in your browser's local storage, remembering which section you were last reading. It never leaves your device.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — accounts and subscription records.
  • Stripe — payments, billing and the customer portal.
  • Cloudflare — serving the site and enforcing access.
  • Resend — sending sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the freely available part of the site.

Each holds only what it needs for its job. We do not send your data anywhere else.

One outbound request

While you view a fight card, your browser fetches current prices directly from Polymarket, a public market data source, so that prices update without reloading. That request comes from your browser and is subject to their privacy practices. It carries no information about you or your account.

How long we keep things

Account and subscription records are kept while your account exists, and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting rules require us to retain payment records. Edge server logs are short-lived and retained by Cloudflare under their own policy.

Your rights

Email support@octanealpha.com and we will, within 30 days: tell you what we hold about you, send you a copy, correct anything wrong, or delete your account and its data. Deletion is permanent, and we may need to retain payment records where the law requires it.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under GDPR, the CCPA or similar laws. We apply the above to everyone rather than only where it is compulsory.

Children

This service is not for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a minor has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change what we collect or who processes it, we will update this page and email active subscribers before the change takes effect.
