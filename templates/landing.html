Method & round

Not just who wins — how, and when

Method gridevery fight

Every fight carries a full method distribution and a round, not a single side. A KO/TKO read and a decision read on the same fighter are completely different bets at completely different prices — and the props board is where the mispricing usually is.

The distribution is coherent with the round curve by construction: the chance a fight ends early and the chance it goes the distance are the same number seen from two directions, so they cannot contradict each other.

●Most services pick a side and stop. Method and round need an actual model of how the fight plays out, not a lean on who is better.
